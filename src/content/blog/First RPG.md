### The Memory

The first memory of playing an RPG, really RPGs in general, is playing a game with my younger brother in the back of our mothers station wagon, we were too young to sit in the rear facing seats. This couldn't have been my first experience with RPGs, and I am sure our older brother told us stories about his D&D games, I just don't remember them.

I vividly remember sitting in the back of the station wagon, waiting in a parking lot as our mother ran a quick errand. I remember which one and roughly where in the lot. My younger brother and I sat in the back, a pad of lined paper, a set of four [Pilot Precise V5](https://powertothepen.com/pens/precise-v5/) pens, black, blue, green, and red, and a d6 or two. 

I don't remember anything of the rules, I don't know if we had firm rules or just vibes based of the stories of D&D we'd heard. 

I remember we'd draw part of a dungeon, each line on the paper was a level, we'd fill it with traps and doors. We'd roll dice for reasons that I do not remember.

#### Reminiscing

Today I'd say we were playing a [Free Kriegsspiel Revolution (FKR)](https://d66kobolds.blogspot.com/2020/09/free-kriegsspiel-worlds-not-rules-etc.html) game, but at the time we were just playing. 

We had an amazing time!
