### The Memory

I got [Star Wars Missions 1: Assault on Yavin Four](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Star_Wars_Missions_1:_Assault_on_Yavin_Four) at a Scholastic book fair in 3rd grade. I didn't really know what it was, but I saw a black box with Star Wars on it and knew I wanted it! 

Sitting on the living room couch, I devoured the book, my father finally having to send me to bed. From what I recall, you played as a rebel on Yavin after the Death Star was destroyed; the details are fuzzy at best. I remember that the book ended on a cliffhanger with a stormtrooper holding a blaster at your head and you discovering the Death Star had been transporting large amounts of contaminated Bacta. 

#### Reminiscing

The book had both choose your own adventure components and dice rolling to determine certain outcomes. I haven't run across that combo in anything else, though I am sure it exists. I question that, given I've played the board game [Tales of the Arabian Nights](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgame/34119/tales-of-the-arabian-nights) springs to mind. As I think about it more, I come back to the idea of an adventure for a group done in that style.
