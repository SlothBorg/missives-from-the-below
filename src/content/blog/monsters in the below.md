There are no vast councils of Dragons, no mated pair guarding their nest of eggs in the desert.

There is just **the** Dragon.

To steal the parlance of D&D (3rd ed and on), the density and number of "monsters" decreases, near exponentially, by Challenge Rating (CR) to a limit of 1.
