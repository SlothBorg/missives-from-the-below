### Dwarves
#### Stone Sight

You eat a gem to gain darkvision for a watch per 100gp in value of the gem.

If the gem is part of an item, the item is notched. If it is magical check the Miscast / Maleficence Table.

This subverts the idea of no darkvision, but also gives players a painful choice in a moment where it might matter and I like that! Naturally Dwarves who consume too many gems become addicted to them. 

### Elves
#### Unnatural Beauty

*You're grace and beauty calm and incite envy in equal measure*

Favor on disposition tests, all failures on disposition tests are great failures.
