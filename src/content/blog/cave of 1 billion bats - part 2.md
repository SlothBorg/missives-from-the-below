## A Note on the System Used

I used a stripped down version of His Majesty the Worm to run this, as a bit of a secrete playtest of a hack of HMtW I am working on.

### TL:DR

Quick notes on the rules I used:

* Characters have 4 attributes, Swords, Pentacles, Cups and Wands, attributes are rated 1 - 4
* The target number is 14, advantage grants +2 disadvantage -2. Players draw a card from the minor arcana deck, met or beat 14. Add your corresponding attribute based on the action.
* Weapons do 1 - 2 damage, paired down spell list.
* Light armor has 1 dot, that can be damaged to negate damage, medium 2, heavy 3, light shields 1, heavy 2, and helms 1. 
* Players have 3 health conditions, Staggered, Injured and Death's Door. effectively 3 HP.


### Converting the Adventure

The only thing I needed to adjust was damage, in HMtW weapons do 1 maybe 2 wounds. Any attack that used more than a d6 damage did two wounds, anything less did 1 wound.

2d4 would be 1 wound, 1d8 would be 2, it could use some refinement but as a quick rule it worked. 

## There Was Blood! There Was Carnage!

Most of the folks I ran this for had some experience with non 5e games, but most were most familiar with 5th ed. So I made a point to explain that this was lethal and characters would die! When they died they were welcome to make another character or tag someone else in. 

Players took wounds in the first bat swarm, they had decided everyone should have a light in case they split up. Spoiler they never split up.
