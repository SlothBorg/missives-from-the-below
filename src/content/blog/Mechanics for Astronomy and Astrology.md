## Lore Dump

In the modern day Astronomy and Astrology are separate fields the first a rigorous science the second a belief system in which, a select subset of, celestial bodies influence or determine matters of our lives.

That description alone should give a good indication on where I fall on a spectrum of Astronomy to Astrology.

HOWEVER, this isn't about the modern day, in **The City** the differentiation between Astronomy and Astrology has not yet happened. Learned folk make no real distinction, and those who study the stars and chart the movements of the celestial bodies may use the terms interchangeably or have one they believe is right and proper.

The [Rider–Waite Tarot deck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rider%E2%80%93Waite_Tarot), probably the one most folks are familiar with, is heavily linked to certain esoteric astrological practices. Each card is tied to one of the five planes visible via the naked eye planet as well as one of the astrological signs. For example, the following cards are linked to the [constellation Leo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leo_(constellation)):

| Suit | Card Name | Element | Planet | Sign | 
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Major Arcana | Strength | Fire | Sun | Leo|
| Major Arcana | The Sun | Fire | Sun | Leo|
| Minor Arcana | Five of Wands | Fire | Sun | Leo|
| Minor Arcana | Six of Wands | Fire | Sun | Leo|
| Minor Arcana | Seven of Wands | Fire | Sun | Leo|

Cards are additionally tied to [alchemical metals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Classical_planet#Alchemy), via the plants but that is something to explore on another day.
