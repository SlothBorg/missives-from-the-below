## Digging this dungeon as I explore it!

Herein are my notes, ideas, and mad ramblings about dungeons, the world of my home game(s), and RPG design. The latter is *currently mainly* focused around a hack of [His Majesty the Worm](https://www.hismajestytheworm.games/).

This project is, more or less, broken down into the following categories: 

### [Setting](/tags/setting/)

Made up of **The City**, **The Below**, **The Province**, **The Wild**, **The Waste**, and **The Above**

#### Setting Design Constraints

* No inventing new words.
  * Names of people are fine.
  * Places are iffy; prefer generic or diadactic names, e.g., Istanbul means "the city" or "in the city" referring to Constantinople.
* The large areas are **The City**, **The Below**, **The Province**, **The Wild**, **The Waste**, and **The Above**

### [System Design](/tags/system/)

#### System Design Constraints 

* Uses a tarot deck.
* Worldbuilding through tables and the names of items, spells, abilities, etc.
* The character sheet should be minimal; it does not hold the answers.
* Different archetypes should feel different!

### [Thoughts](/tags/thoughts/)

Musings on RPGs, memories of RPGs I've played etc.
